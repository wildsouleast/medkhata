About us

Made for the medical store counter

Dawalekh is a simple tool that helps small medical stores and clinics in India stop losing money to expired stock, missed reorders and forgotten refills.

Why we built Dawalekh

Most neighbourhood medical stores still track stock in a register or in their memory. Expiry dates are checked only now and then, fast-moving medicines run out without warning, and regular patients forget their refills. Big pharmacy software is often costly, complicated and made for computers.

Dawalekh is built in India by a B.Pharm student who understands how a medical store works. The idea is simple: one clean app on the phone that tells the store owner, every morning, what needs attention today.

What we believe

  • Simple beats complicated. Big buttons, clear colours and only the features a small store really uses.
  • Phone first. No computer, no installation, no training needed.
  • Honest pricing. A free trial, monthly plans and no auto-debit.
  • Your data is yours. We never sell it, and you can download it anytime.
