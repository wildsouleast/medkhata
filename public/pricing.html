Billing questions

How does billing work?

Each payment gives you 30 days of your chosen plan. If you pay during your free trial, the 30 days start after the trial ends, so you don't lose any free time.

Which payment methods can I use?

UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking, all through Razorpay's secure checkout.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. Pay for the new plan from the Plan page in the app. It applies from the moment of payment and adds 30 days to your account.

Will I get a reminder before my plan ends?

Yes. The app shows how many days are left on your plan and reminds you to renew as the end date comes near.
