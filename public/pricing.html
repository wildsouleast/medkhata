Billing questions

How does billing work?

Pro monthly (₹149) gives you 30 days and Pro yearly (₹1,499) gives you 365 days per payment. Basic (₹49) gives you 30 days. If you pay during your free trial, the paid days start after the trial ends, so you don't lose any free time. If you don't pay, your store becomes read-only: you can see and download everything, but you can't add new bills or purchases.

Which payment methods can I use?

UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking, all through Razorpay's secure checkout.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Pay for the one you want from the Plan page in the app; its days are added to your account.

What happens when my trial or plan ends?

Your store becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted: you can still open your stock, old bills and udhaar, and download them as CSV. To make new bills or purchases again, pay for Basic or Pro from the Plan page; everything opens immediately.

Will I get a reminder before my plan ends?

Yes. The app shows how many days are left on your plan and reminds you to renew as the end date comes near.
