New accounts get a 7-day free trial with all Pro features. No payment details are needed for the trial. There is no free plan after the trial. When the trial ends (or a paid plan ends) without a new payment, the account becomes read-only: you can still view and download your data, but you cannot add new bills, purchases or other changes until you buy a plan. Nothing is deleted when an account becomes read-only. Your data is kept as described in our Privacy Policy.
