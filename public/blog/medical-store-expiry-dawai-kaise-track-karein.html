Medical store mein expiry dawai kaise track karein

Dawalekh team · Updated 25 October 2026 · 6 min read

Har medical store ka ek chhupa hua kharcha hota hai: expiry wali dawai. Strip shelf ke peeche pada rehta hai, aur pata tab chalta hai jab return karne ka time nikal chuka hota hai. Achhi baat ye hai ki thode se system se ye nuksaan kaafi kam ho sakta hai. Ye guide register, Excel ya app, kisi bhi tareeke ke saath kaam karti hai.

1. Har dawai ka batch aur expiry likhna shuru karein

Sirf dawai ka naam kaafi nahi hai. Ek hi dawai ke do batch alag mahine mein expire ho sakte hain. Maal aate hi har item ke liye ye likhein:

  • Dawai ka naam aur strength (jaise Pantoprazole 40 mg)
  • Batch number
  • Expiry month/year (strip ya carton pe likha hota hai)
  • Quantity (strip/bottle) aur supplier ka naam

Tip: Shuru mein poora stock ek saath mat likhiye. Pehle mehngi aur tez bikne wali dawaiyan likhein, phir har din thoda aur jodte jaayein.

2. Expiry ko 90, 60 aur 30 din ke buckets mein dekhein

Ek lambi list se kuch samajh nahi aata. Use teen hisson mein baantiye:

  • 90 din ke andar: in dawaiyon ko aage rakhiye aur pehle bechiye.
  • 60 din ke andar: distributor se return ya exchange ki baat shuru kariye.
  • 30 din ke andar: turant faisla: return, exchange, ya alag rakhna.

Register mein ye kaam mahine mein ek baar poore stock ko dekh ke hota hai. Excel mein expiry column pe filter/sort se hota hai. Dawalekh jaisa app ye buckets apne aap dikhata hai.

3. FEFO follow karein: First Expiry, First Out

Jo batch pehle expire hoga, wahi pehle bikna chahiye. Shelf pe naya maal peeche aur purana (jaldi expiry wala) aage rakhiye. Counter staff ko bhi ye niyam batayein, kyunki naya carton kholna aasaan lagta hai par nuksaan wahin se hota hai.

4. Distributor return ka time mat chookiye

Bahut se distributor aur company expiry ya near-expiry maal ka return, credit note ya exchange dete hain, lekin har ek ki shartein alag hoti hain (kitne din pehle, kitna percent, bill ki copy). Apne har bade supplier se unki return policy pooch ke likh lijiye, aur 60-90 din wali list unke saath time pe share kariye.

Return ke niyam supplier aur company ke hisaab se badalte hain. Yahan diye gaye din sirf ek achhi aadat ka example hain, koi sarkari niyam nahi.

5. Expired dawai ko turant alag kariye

Expired dawai bechna kanoon ke khilaf hai aur mareez ke liye khatarnaak bhi ho sakta hai. Expired stock ko ek alag dabbe mein rakhein, us pe saaf likhein "EXPIRED: NOT FOR SALE", aur use bikri wali shelf se door rakhein. Uska hisaab bhi alag rakhein, taaki stock value sahi dikhe.

6. Low stock aur expiry ek saath dekhein

Expiry se bachne ka doosra tareeka hai zaroorat se zyada order na karna. Dheere bikne wali dawai ki chhoti quantity mangwaayein. Jab kisi dawai ka stock ek minimum se neeche jaaye, tabhi order kariye.

7. Har hafte 10 minute ka review

Hafte mein ek din (jaise Monday subah) 10 minute nikaal ke dekhiye: kya expire hone wala hai, kya khatam hone wala hai, aur kis supplier ko return bhejna hai. Ye ek aadat hi sabse zyada paisa bachati hai.
