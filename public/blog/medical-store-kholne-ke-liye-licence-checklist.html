Medical store kholne ke liye licence aur software checklist

Dawalekh team · Updated 25 October 2026 · 7 min read

Zaroori: Ye ek general guide hai, legal salah nahi. Drug licence aapke state ka Drugs Control Department deta hai, aur fees, documents aur process state ke hisaab se badal sakte hain. Apply karne se pehle apne state ke drug controller ki official website ya office se current niyam zaroor confirm karein.

1. Drug licence: kaun sa form?

India mein dawai bechne ka licence Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 aur Drugs Rules, 1945 ke under milta hai. Retail medical store ke liye aam taur pe:

  • Form 19: retail sale licence ke liye application
  • Form 20: retail licence, Schedule C/C1 ke alawa wali dawaiyon ke liye
  • Form 21: retail licence, Schedule C/C1 dawaiyon ke liye (jaise kuch injectables, vaccines, sera)

Wholesale ke liye alag licence (Form 20B / 21B) hote hain. Kai states ab online apply karwaate hain, kuch central ONDLS portal pe aur kuch apne portal pe.

2. Registered pharmacist

Retail sale "registered pharmacist" ki dekh-rekh mein honi chahiye. Iska matlab State Pharmacy Council mein registered pharmacist (D.Pharm/B.Pharm ke baad registration). Agar aap khud pharmacist nahi hain, to ek registered pharmacist ko rakhna padega, aur unke documents (registration certificate, consent/appointment letter) application mein lagte hain.

3. Dukaan ki zaroori shartein

  • Jagah: retail ke liye aam taur pe kam se kam 10 square metre, aur retail + wholesale ek saath ho to zyada (aksar 15 sq m). Apne state ka niyam check karein.
  • Fridge: jin dawaiyon ko thanda rakhna hota hai (insulin, kuch vaccines), unke liye refrigerator.
  • Saaf, sukhi jagah aur dawaiyon ko dhoop/nami se bachane ka intezaam.
  • Dukaan ka rent agreement ya ownership proof, site plan/layout.

4. Documents jo aam taur pe maange jaate hain

  • Application form aur state ki tay fees ki receipt
  • Owner/partners ka ID aur address proof
  • Dukaan ka ownership ya rent proof aur layout plan
  • Pharmacist ka registration certificate aur consent letter
  • Affidavit/declaration (state ke format mein)

5. Baaki registrations

  • GST: turnover limit se upar hone par zaroori; kai distributor GST number maangte hain, isliye bahut se store shuru mein hi le lete hain.
  • Shop & Establishment registration (state ke niyam ke hisaab se)
  • Udyam (MSME) registration: optional, par free hai aur kai jagah kaam aata hai

6. Dukaan khulne ke baad ke records

  • Schedule H1 register: H1 dawaiyon (kuch antibiotics, anti-TB, habit-forming dawaiyan) ki bikri pe mareez ka naam, doctor ka naam aur quantity likhni hoti hai, aur ye record kam se kam 3 saal rakhna hota hai.
  • Kharid ke bill (purchase invoices) sambhal ke rakhein.
  • Expiry wali dawai ko alag rakhna aur bechna nahi.

7. Software checklist: pehle din se kya chahiye

  • Billing: customer bill ke liye billing software ya bill book. (Dawalekh me simple bill banta hai, print ya WhatsApp pe; GST return filing ke liye CA/accounting software alag chahiye.)
  • Stock aur expiry: har dawai ka batch aur expiry, 90/60/30 din alert, low-stock alert. (Dawalekh isi kaam ke liye bana hai.)
  • Backup: data cloud pe ho, taaki phone/computer kharab hone pe kuch na khoye.
  • Regular mareez: refill reminder (WhatsApp pe) se mareez wapas aate hain.

Expiry ka system shuru se kaise banayein, ye padhein: Expiry dawai kaise track karein.
